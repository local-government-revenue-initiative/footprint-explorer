    Screening tool. Counts come from modelled footprints (Overture merges Microsoft, Google and OSM sources) and are therefore not 100% accurate. Buildings are kept when their centroid falls inside the zone; footprints are not cut at the zone edge. Height and floors (height ÷ 3 m, minimum 1) are shown only when at least 20% of the buildings have a height in Overture.

    Notes

    How it works

    The page sends the bounding box of your zone to a small Python service that reads Overture Maps with the overturemaps package and returns the footprints. Imagery: Esri World Imagery. Search: OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Statistics follow the Python scripts (taxable threshold, size categories, floors = height ÷ 3).
